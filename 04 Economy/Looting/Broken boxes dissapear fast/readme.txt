-Module:
Broken boxes dissapear fast

----------------------------------------------------------
-Compatibility:

----------------------------------------------------------
-Explanation:

When you break a lootable box, the "bones" remain for as long as a minute. Maybe you want them gone faster for performance reasons, or the pieces stop you from taking the loot if badly positioned.
This is intended to make those broken pieces go away as soon as you break the boxes.
