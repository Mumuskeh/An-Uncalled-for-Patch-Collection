-Module:
Lootboxes [ahuyn]

----------------------------------------------------------
-Compatibility:
These are simple additions and should be compatible with any other patches.

Overhauls, if there are any, are an unknown.

----------------------------------------------------------
-Explanation:

New "Pelican Sub-Machinegun Case" will carry SMGs, which populated pools very sparsely (the "Old weapon crate" could have the the PPSH41, and the Monolith cache could have the P90 and the MP7, but no other SMGs were made available anywhere).
All non unique SMGs will be present in it. The MP5SD will be slightly rarer than the MP5, and the MP7 and P90 will be rarer than the rest. Carbines will also be possible, but they will be rarer than most SMGs.
The AK-74UNB will be a unique prize.

The normal "Pelican Rifle case" will now also carry modern shotguns such as SPAS, Saiga or USAS.

The normal "Old weapon crate", "Army weapon crate", "Pelican Rifle case", and "Monolith Cache" may also carry, very rarely, appropiate LMGs.
