- The script is no longer looking for animations (we avoid console warnings).
- Deleted configs and the script controlling the addon's MCM section.
- Commented out redundant (but definitely still some left) code, and edited the local simple_mode a bit.

The original mod may not be needed but this hasn't been tested 100%.
