-Explanation:

The modules for each mod have been gathered in the same folder.
There's an optional merge of the separate modules to reduce the number of files used; text, corpse looting, and dialogue files were left alone.
Place the contents of all three original mods into the same folder, then proceed with the merge patch as with the individual ones.

Minor Meds are excluded from the last two. Who's gonna reward you with a goose chase for some paracetamol?
Faction Medkits is also excluded from the Package mod, as I can't see a way to differentiate by faction.

For both GSO and Lootboxes integration, Pressure bandages will be one tier rarer than tourniquets, and cooling bandages one over vanilla bandages. 
For GSO, along adding the new items to the system, it will reduce the rarity of the vanilla medkits by one rarity tier, to make some of the new medkits actually more rare or common as needed.
For Lootboxes, if you were complaining about getting too many bandages and standard medkits, their rarity has been increased a bit. In exchange, prepare for the RAGSPOCALIPSE, with a side of bandaids and a seasoning of civilian medkits.

It would be neat to make a certain per faction/area loot chance for the faction medkits. Will look in the future.
