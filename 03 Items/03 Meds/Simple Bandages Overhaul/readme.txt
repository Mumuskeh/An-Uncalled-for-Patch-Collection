-Module:
Simple Bandage Overhaul

----------------------------------------------------------
-Compatibility:

Compatible with other medical items tweaks such as
DLTX MiniMod's Medical Items Revision and Medkits Radiation Revision
Health Rebalance
Drugs That Make Sense
Jivix's Easier Anomaly - Medical module

If using the main EFT Medical AFAK module in this package, let it overwrite these files.

----------------------------------------------------------
-Explanation:

Most medical item overhauls focus on the medkits and the drugs, but barely on the bandages, tourniquets and similar items. This will tweak a variety of those items, vanilla or modded:
	-Vanilla bandages and tourniquets
	-New Medkits and Bandages
	-Drugs That Make Sense (Update 9.1)
	-Hunter Survival Crafting
	-STALKER 2 HoC Cons Meds Pack
	-EFT Medical - Aluminum Splint
	-EFT Medical - Esmarch tourniquet
	-EFT Medical - Splint
	-EFT Medical - AFAK

Files are divided in two folders: bandages and tourniquets.


Bandages will be cheaper, so that curing a bleed is easier in the early game, but you won't be able to sell them for a fortune later after looting everyone and their mother.
All these items won't cause hunger, thirst, or sleep.


Tourniquets will be much more expensive and rarer; however, they take full effect a couple seconds faster.
In addition, if More Drops from Dugs (DLTX Minimod) is present, tourniquets may be cleaned for reuse.
When using a vanilla tourniquet, you'll get a "used" one. It will have a reduced effect and cause a health loss (very small, but there has to be some reason to not using it), and be heavily devalued (who'd want to buy your bloodied plastic strip?).
However, you can combine it with water or alcohol to get a "cleaned" copy for further use.
The cleaned one will be just like a fresh tourniquet, just with half the value.
Patches included for New Food (DLTX Minimod) and Fillable Canteens.


The covered mods will have these particular changes:

-New Medkits and Bandages:
Cooling and pressure bandage are direct upgrades over vanilla bandages, both dealing better with bleeding. To differentiate them a bit, now cooling will take full effect over a second longer than the standard bandage, while pressure will do over two seconds less.
Bandaids will now have 20 instead of 6 uses and be reduced an item tier to 1. This shouldn't be unbalanced, due to how little help they are.
Rags now won't be as bad dealing with bleeding anymore. Half the damage it caused now it will be over time, to prevent a fast death if used in desperation when at the brink.

-Drugs That Make Sense (Update 9.1):
Aloe Vera Bandage will treat both bleeding and and provide fire damage resistance, both effects spread out for a long time. It'll be more useful for traveling than actual combat.

-Hunter Survival Crafting:
Crafted bandages will be the same as the rags in New Medkits and Bandages, but without causing health damage.

-STALKER 2 HoC Cons Meds Pack:
S2 bandages won't be any dfferent from vanilla ones (for now).

-EFT Medical:
The AFAK will act like normal bandages as per its animation, but it will be treated as a bag of bandages with 10 uses, weighted and priced appropiately.
The normal splint will be relatively cheap, but won't help much. Splints aren't really meant for hemorrages, even if they can support other methods with it. So without something like BHS, there's no really much point in them.
The aluminium splint will be somewhat better, and have ten uses (it's a roll that you can cut).
