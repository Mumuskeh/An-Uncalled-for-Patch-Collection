-Explanation:
Fixes the icons to work in DX8-9, and gathers them up in the same file.

The aloe vera bandage only had a long lasting fire resistance effect, now it also has a small bleed heal effect (half that of a normal bandage), and gave it a price as it lacked one. It should make it a good, very long lasting middle ground betwen vanilla and No_Data's bandages, fit for long treks in non-threatening territory and saving your superior resources.
Its internal ID has also been renamed, so it's ordered better in the inventory with other bandages.

Added basic animation settings.

Integrated into the vanilla rewards, looting, and stash systems.

Integration into:
Grok's Stash Overhaul
Lootboxes
TB's RF Receiver Hidden Package Sidequests + CookBook's QOL patch (requires the module in this package)
