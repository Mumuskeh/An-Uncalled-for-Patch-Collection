-Module:
New Food For Anomaly (DLTX Minimod) [DAN_LUKA, Demonized]

----------------------------------------------------------
-Compatibility:
NOTE: You need the original mod. Disable the textures/ui folder, and the files mod_system_eda_icons.ltx and items_denro.ltx

----------------------------------------------------------
-Explanation:
In case you don't use the remaster. As icon textures go, the vanilla ui_icon_equipment.dds isn't a small file. In fact it is pretty big. This mod adds its icons with a second, possibly outdated ui_icon_equipment. This is ridiculous for us potato players who worry when the inventory freezes when opening it.

Now it uses it's own, small, reorganized icon file. And holy crap, it really needed that reorganization. Icons sharpened up a little bit.

Also consolidated the config files for items and icons into a single DLTX one.
