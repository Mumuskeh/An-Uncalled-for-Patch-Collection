-Module:
More Food from Denro - Full Remaster [seven_or_9]

----------------------------------------------------------
-Compatibility:
NOTE: You need the original mod. Disable the mod_system_denro_icons.ltx file and textures/ui folder, and make sure this patch loads later.

Of the extras in the original mod, do not use the Western Goods addon as it is outdated, and the Unpackable MREs is known to cause bugs. 

This and the New Food module in DLTX Minimod are basically the same mod.
New Food seems a bit better balanced, even if keeping more items from the original mod. I merely add this here as an alternative.

----------------------------------------------------------
-Explanation:
Configs file spring cleaned a bit.
Streamlined trade files.
Icons moved into a file of their own, sharpened up a little bit, and reorganized.
