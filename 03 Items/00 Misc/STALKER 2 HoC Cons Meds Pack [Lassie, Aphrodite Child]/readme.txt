----------------------------------------------------------
-Explanation:

Properly DLTX'd the items.

The old bread is turned into "fresh", so I improve its benefits compared to normal bread, and make it so Food Poisoning doesn't act on it.
The mineral water was being replaed by the data of both the limited edition energy drink and the water. The normal water will now be replaced by the LE energy drink, with the same benefits as the normal energy drink.

The milk is the only HoC item that doesn't replace anything, but some files list all items as if they didn't replace anything. This may lead to issues. So, the craft file is removed, and we disable all items except the milk in the the item rewards and dynamic item spawn files

Some items weren't receiving the new icons. Now they do.
Gathered all icons into a single file.

For the "No replacer" files, the item names won't be tagged with "(HoC)".

Trader distribution. As in vanilla and besides exceptions like Sidorovich, traders won't deal with food or meds when a suitable medic or barman is nearby (ie: Sakharov and Spirit), or not at all (ie: medics won't sell food, and barmen and Flea Market merchants won't sell drugs except bandages, dedicated gear merchants won't sell either).
Traders will only stock at the lowest goodwill tier (the common vanilla behaviour for drugs and food. No real need for any more as the mod did).
Fixed some drug entries for Hermann's inventory.


Added configs for actor effects, treasure manager, death loot.

Integration into:
Food Poisoning. It will act upon the spoiled can, but not the old bread (now fresh bread).
Grok's Stash Overhaul
Lootboxes
