Makes some unique NPCs tougher and have unique loot to drop. Delete /scripts to remove bonus npc toughness
New item added in, partial basic toolkit parts.  Dropped from technicians.  Needs Hunter Survival Crafting loaded before this addon.

Maps now drop from certain NPCs.  Such as:

Stribog drops a partial Red Forest map, letting you reach Jupiter or Army Warehouse.
The Doc in Great Swamp will drop a partial Agroprom map that gives access to the underground.
General Voronin in Rostok will now always drop an Army Warehouse map and partial Radar map to Lab x19.
Lukash in Army Warehouse also drops an Army Warehouse map and partial Radar map to Lab x19.
Sultan will drop the Truck Cemetary map and strelok's notes to reach x18.
Deadcity guide will drop 3 maps: Dead city, Limansk, and partial Red Forest.
The Outskirts guide drops a Jupiter Underground map.
Eidolon drops four maps.  All the routes needed to reach the CNPP and Generators.
Griffin in Outskirts drops the partial Radar to Pripyat map.
