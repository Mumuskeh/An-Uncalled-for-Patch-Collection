-Module:
Hiddant's Tweaks & Fixes [Hiddant]
https://www.moddb.com/mods/stalker-anomaly/addons/hiddants-tweaks-fixes-for-anomaly-15

----------------------------------------------------------
-Compatibility:

The original mod is not needed.

Other similar modules in this pack are compatible. Those will have higher load priority.

----------------------------------------------------------
-Explanation:

DLTX conversion of all modules.
The "Rework of hybrid and Clear Sky Seva suits" tweak has been split in three different files, one for each suit, in case you want one but not the others.


Original descriptions:

- Increase spare parts prices (x2 higher) :
	It makes all parts worth selling. Selling a whole armor or weapon should still bring more value.

- Increase non-edible mutant parts prices (x1.5 higher) :
	I recommend using it to make mutant hunting more rewarding.

- Increase non-edible mutant parts prices (x2.5 higher) :
	I recommend using it with the ammo price tweak to keep a correct balance between hunting mutants and the money you get from them.

- Increase ammo price (x2 higher) :
	Ammo should be valuable, in the Zone, as clean imported food should be.
	Gauss rifle ammo price is only increased by 1.5 for "clean" and "Cardan" versions. It is still overkill to shoot it, in most scenarios.

- GP-5 and PBF masks values changed and masks weights reduced :
	Both of these masks use filters with the same protection rating : CBRN.
	Though, the PBF mask is more expensive in real life (A bit more technologically advanced).
	The version of the PBF used in the first games with the large visor doesn't exist in real life, so I just assume it is an old prototype, less effective.
	For balance reasons, I set the stats of the GP5 mask lower than the PBF.
	All of the weight values of gas masks have been lowered to their real weight. As I haven't found any test on the M40 mask weight, I made it close to the M50 model.

- Rework of hybrid and Clear Sky Seva suits :
	The hybrid suit is supposed to be a budget Seva suit. Though, it's actually more expensive and capable.
   I have set his price a little lower than the regular Seva, slightly reduced its radiation protection and increased its rupture protection.
   Its additional carryweight has been reduced and I have removed one artifact pouch to equal the classic Seva suit.
	The regular Clear Sky Seva suit received a buff to its stats to match the description. I have slightly reduced its radiation protection value, though.
   The price has been increased and the additionnal carryweight reduced.
	I also found that the Pathfinder variant was way too unbalanced, so I reduced its protection against radiation and increased its damage resistances.

- Custom loot drops :
	Looting very bad condition weapons all the time felt too annoying and grindy to me. Below are the new values. I tried to balance it so you can still loot bad quality weapons.

	novice			= 0.1,	0.4
	trainee			= 0.15,	0.45
	experienced		= 0.2,	0.5
	professional		= 0.25,	0.55
	veteran			= 0.3,	0.6
	expert			= 0.5,	0.75
	master			= 0.6,	0.9
	legend			= 0.7,	0.95

- Repair kits and misc tweak :
	I've reworked the repair system. I did this to make every item worth buying or using. Now, every low condition item that can't be disassembled can also be repaired.
	The costs have consequently been adjusted and balanced.
	I have also added the bandana and balaclava to the list of repair parts for each item already accepting the cloth mask.
	Below is a list of minimum conditions repair, cleaning kits and glues can now repair and their efficiency.

	Glues :
	- "Zviezda" glue tube -> minimum 93% condition, condition gain + 3%.
	- "Tur"     glue tube -> minimum 90% condition, condition gain + 4%.
	- "M17"     glue tube -> minimum 87% condition, condition gain + 5%.

	Emergency armor repair kit (duct tape and cloth icon) -> minimum 82% condition, condition gain + 6%.

	Sewing kits :
	- Basic    sewing kit -> minimum 75% condition, condition gain + 6%.
	- Advanced sewing kit -> minimum 80% condition, condition gain + 6%.
	- Heavy    sewing kit -> minimum 85% condition, condition gain + 6%.

	Cleaning kits :
	- All specific calibers cleaning kits -> minimum 65% condition, condition gain 8%.
	- Universal             cleaning kit  -> minimum 75% condition, condition gain unchanged. (Lower efficiency, more versatility).

	Gun oils :
	- "Rusak" gun oil                      -> minimum 90% condition, condition gain + 3%.
	- "Dvojka" gun oil and spray lubricant -> minimum 90% condition, condition gain + 7%.
	- "Brunox gun care" spray lubricant    -> minimum 85% condition, condition gain + 5%.
	- Hoppe's "No. 9" cleaning solvent     -> minimum 80% condition, condition gain + 8%.

	Repair kits :
	- Reduced the repair bonus to every repair kit to 7%, so it makes replacement parts more important.
	- Headgear repair kit -> minimum 0% condition. (Headgears can't be disassembled, so you can repair them at any condition now).

	Sharpening stone :
	- Minimum 0% condition, lowered price, condition gain unchanged. (Knifes can't be disassembled, so you can repair them at any condition now).

	Misc :
	- Balanced the price of industrial grease, steel wool and duct tape.
