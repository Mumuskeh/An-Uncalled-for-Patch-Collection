-Compatibility:
Original mod not required.

----------------------------------------------------------
-Explanation:
Moves the edited strings to files of their own for better compatibility.

Load order is whatever, though I tried to rename the new files so they are loaded immediately after vanilla files, but before mod files, so that if any mod touches the same texts, they have priority.

For example, Krepin's MP7, which is chambered for 4.6, but THIGHS mention the vanilla behavior of being modified for 9mm.

All patches are also worked out, except the BHS one. BHS Realistic Overhaul already does the same, and BHS Rework doesn't need it.
