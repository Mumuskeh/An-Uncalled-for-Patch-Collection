This add-on is designed to give to Cordon Rookie Village's NPCs some personality in their equipment and increases their firepower.

Cordon Rookie Village is one of the most iconic locations in S.T.A.L.K.E.R. franchise, with some unique NPCs in the game. Sidorovich, the main trader of the zone, Wolf, the main rookie stalker guide and Hip, the only female stalker in the game.

Unfortunately, considering the fighter NPCs, except by Wolf, all other characters have no personality in their equipment. Fanatic uses a simple AKS-74UN and a Makarov PM, Hip uses just a simple Makarov PM and is usually killed by friend fire after long gameplay.

This add-on solves this problem turning Hip invulnerable if player is Loner's friend and Warfare is disabled (like Fanatic), giving to her a M4A1 and giving to Fanatic an AKS-74UN Camouflaged.

This changes sightly increases the village firepower and gives this place a great personality, as it deserves.


**INSTALLATION**

Just copy the *gamedata* folder to your S.T.A.L.K.E.R. Anomaly folder.

Obs.: To change the NPCs loadout, a new game is necessary, but Hip's invulnerability don't.


**INCOMPATIBILITIES**

Any mod that changes cordon's NPCs loadout or Hip script.

I.E.: Any add-on that changes *character_desc_escape.xml* or *devushka.ltx* will conflict with this add-on.
