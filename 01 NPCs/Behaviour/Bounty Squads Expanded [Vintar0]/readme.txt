----------------------------------------------------------
-Compatibility:
NOTE: Original mode is still required. Let this overwrite it.

----------------------------------------------------------
-Explanation:

Assigns Meadow as a safe level, due to the inability of NPCs to enter/exit the area.

Slightly increases the number of possible squads going after the player per rank:
Ranl		vanilla	|	BSE	|	Uncalled
-------------------------------------------
novice			0	|	0	|	0
trainee			0	|	0	|	0
experienced		0	|	0	|	1
professional	0	|	1	|	1
veteran			1	|	1	|	2
expert			1	|	1	|	2
master			2	|	2	|	3
legend			2	|	3	|	3


Optionally, you may disable the limitation to attack you if you're sorrounded by friendly NPCs.
